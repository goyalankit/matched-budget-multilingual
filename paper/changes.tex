\section*{Outcome of the previous submission}

This paper was submitted to the August 2026 ARR cycle. It was desk rejected
before review for exceeding the eight-page content limit for long papers. No
reviews were issued, so there are no reviewer comments to respond to. This
document therefore describes the change made to address the desk rejection
itself.

\section*{The change}

The only substantive change is length. Main content, measured from the title
block to the Limitations section, was reduced from 8.14 pages to 7.79 pages. It
now ends on page 8, within the limit, with margin remaining.

\section*{What did not change}

No experiment, result, statistic, confidence interval, claim, or qualification
was added, removed, weakened, or strengthened. Specifically:

\begin{itemize}[nosep,leftmargin=1.5em]
  \item Every numeric literal in the source is unchanged. The document contains
        771 before and after, as an identical multiset.
  \item No section, table, figure, or equation was added or removed, and their
        order is unchanged.
  \item No citation or cross-reference was added or removed.
  \item No text was deleted. Material that left the numbered sections was
        relocated, not discarded.
\end{itemize}

\section*{How the reduction was achieved}

\paragraph{Plainer prose.} Long sentences were split and wordy constructions
simplified. The numbered content fell from 3{,}482 to 3{,}285 words while the
sentence count \emph{rose} from 184 to 188, so mean sentence length fell from
22.7 to 21.2 words. The text is shorter because it is simpler, not because it is
denser.

\paragraph{Removal of repetition.} The same takeaway was stated in similar words
in the abstract, the introduction, and the discussion. The redundant statements
were consolidated.

\paragraph{One relocation.} The paragraph reporting the residual
native-vs-translate difference at larger budgets and the five remaining
confounds was moved verbatim from the discussion into the Limitations section,
where it belongs and where it does not count against the page limit. Both
numerical values, all five confounds, and the accompanying citation are
preserved exactly.

\section*{Visibility of the negative result}

Because the reduction touched the discussion, we note explicitly that the
prospectively frozen confirmatory non-rejection at \(B^{*}=1024\) remains stated
in both the abstract and Section~4.1, and that the first listed contribution now
states it directly rather than by implication. The separately frozen family of
six tests that does reject is likewise unchanged. Neither result was moved to an
appendix, and neither was de-emphasised.
